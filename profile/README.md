## Ecofrontiers

Applied research at the intersection of climate, AI, and decentralized infrastructure. Based in France.

**Active projects:**

**[AI Design Atlas](https://aidesignatlas.xyz)**—Institutional design primitives for AI-augmented collective decision-making.

**Coharness**—Task-shape routing infrastructure for agent economies: swarms of AI agents coordinated and priced by the shape of the work. In private development.

**[AqueductX](https://aqueductx.trade)**—Peer-to-peer logistics and finance layer for smallholder commodity producers. Agents aggregate, verify and price lots; intents match and settle them.
